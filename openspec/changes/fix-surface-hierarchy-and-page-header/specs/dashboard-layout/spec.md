# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Every page of the dashboard has one page shell, one heading and one trail, and each surface has one role. A visitor sees where a
page sits in the frame, reads its title, its trail and its description from one left edge, and finds the same breadcrumb on every
page. The anchors that plugins and operators style against keep their names.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Frame, page and card surfaces follow the surface table

The page SHALL be `surface`. The top bar and the navigation rail SHALL be `surface-container`. The content sheet SHALL be `surface`.
A card SHALL be `surface-container-low`. No primitive or view SHALL choose another container step for these surfaces.

#### Scenario: Frame reads apart from the page

- **WHEN** a visitor opens any dashboard page in light mode
- **THEN** the top bar and the navigation rail are drawn in `surface-container`, the page behind them in `surface`, and the contrast
  between the two is recorded in the pull request

#### Scenario: Card default

- **WHEN** a view draws an `MdCard` without a variant
- **THEN** the card is `surface-container-low` with a 1 pixel `outline` edge, and its edge measures at least 3:1 against the surface
  behind it in light and dark mode

### Requirement: Overlay surfaces carry their role and elevation

A menu or a select listbox SHALL be `surface-container` with `shadow-elevation-2`. A side sheet SHALL be `surface-container-low` with
`shadow-elevation-1`. A dialog SHALL be `surface-container-high` with `shadow-elevation-3`. A floating panel SHALL carry an elevation of
at least `shadow-elevation-2`.

#### Scenario: Menus and dialogs

- **WHEN** a menu, a select listbox and a dialog are open
- **THEN** the menu and the listbox are `surface-container` with `shadow-elevation-2`, and the dialog is `surface-container-high` with
  `shadow-elevation-3`

#### Scenario: Floating panel

- **WHEN** a floating panel such as the More panel on the index page is open
- **THEN** it draws an elevation shadow of at least `shadow-elevation-2`


### Requirement: Nested blocks step up one role

A block nested in a card or a panel SHALL take the role one step above its parent in the order `surface-container-lowest`,
`surface-container-low`, `surface-container`, `surface-container-high`, `surface-container-highest`. A nested block SHALL NOT take the
role of its parent and SHALL NOT take a lower role. Nesting SHALL end at `surface-container-high`; a deeper block SHALL be flattened into its parent.

#### Scenario: Same step is rejected

- **WHEN** an audit event row sits inside the audit view's card
- **THEN** the row is one role above the card, and the row's own edge is recorded in the pull request

#### Scenario: Inverted nesting is rejected

- **WHEN** a cleanup policy block or an index block sits inside a panel
- **THEN** the block is not one role below the panel

### Requirement: The content sheet has a visible edge

From 600 pixels the content sheet SHALL draw an edge against the frame. The edge SHALL be a 1 pixel `outline` border, which measures
at least 3:1 against `surface-container` in light and dark mode. `outline-variant` SHALL NOT be used for this edge.

#### Scenario: Sheet edge in light mode

- **WHEN** a visitor opens an Administration page at 1280 pixels in light mode
- **THEN** the edge of the content sheet is visible against the frame, and its contrast is recorded in the pull request

#### Scenario: Sheet edge at compact width

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the same page at 390 pixels
- **THEN** the content sheet has no frame beside it and draws no edge

### Requirement: Dark surface-dim is darker than surface

In dark mode the `surface-dim` role SHALL have a lower relative luminance than the `surface` role, in the default theme and in a theme
generated from an operator seed colour.

#### Scenario: Default dark theme

- **WHEN** the default dark theme is generated from `src/styles/md3/color.css`
- **THEN** `surface-dim` is `#080f16` and `surface` is `#0d141c`, and the test in `src/styles/md3/color.test.js` reads both values

#### Scenario: Seeded dark theme

- **WHEN** an operator sets a seed colour and the dark theme is generated by `src/theme/runtime.js`
- **THEN** the generated `surface-dim` has a lower relative luminance than the generated `surface`

### Requirement: Every page uses the page shell

Every page of the dashboard SHALL render `MdPage` with the `wide` or the `reading` variant. A `reading` page SHALL be centred with a
maximum width of 960 pixels. A `wide` page SHALL take the full width after the side navigation. No view SHALL write its own `container`
class, and no view SHALL set `mx-auto` or `max-w-*` on its page container.

#### Scenario: Reading page is centred

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the repository detail, the account page or a settings form at 1920 pixels
- **THEN** the page column is centred in the window, and its left and right margins are equal

#### Scenario: Wide page takes the width

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the repositories list at 1920 pixels
- **THEN** the page takes the full width after the side navigation, and its page container carries the `wide` variant

#### Scenario: No hand-written container

- **WHEN** the sources of the views are read by a test
- **THEN** no view contains the `container` class outside `MdPage`

### Requirement: One level one heading per page

Every page SHALL have exactly one heading of level one, which names the page. A page header SHALL NOT draw a second heading of level one
or a repeated name at the same level. A card SHALL draw its title as a heading of level two or three, never level one.

#### Scenario: Repository detail

- **WHEN** a visitor opens a repository detail
- **THEN** the page has exactly one `h1`, which is the repository name, and no `h2` repeats the name

#### Scenario: Snippets card

- **WHEN** a visitor opens a page with the snippets card
- **THEN** the card title is a heading of level two or three, and the page has one `h1`

#### Scenario: Index page

- **WHEN** a signed-out visitor opens the index page
- **THEN** the page has exactly one `h1`, and the sign-in heading is a level two heading

### Requirement: Page header aligns title, description and trail

Every page header SHALL be drawn by `MdPageHeader` with the back control, the trail, the one heading, the description and the actions in
their own regions. The back control SHALL sit in its own row above the text column, outside it. The trail, the heading and the
description SHALL share one left edge, which is the left edge of the page container. The text column SHALL NOT move when a back control
appears or disappears.

#### Scenario: Title and trail share an edge at 1280

- **WHEN** a visitor opens a repository detail at 1280 pixels
- **THEN** the left edge of the title and the left edge of the trail are equal within 0.5 pixel, measured from their bounding boxes

#### Scenario: Title and trail share an edge at 1920

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the same page at 1920 pixels
- **THEN** the left edges match within 0.5 pixel, and the title is not at the window's left edge

#### Scenario: Back control outside the text column

- **WHEN** a detail page draws its back control at 390 pixels
- **THEN** the control is a sibling of the text column, its glyph starts at the left edge of the container, and the title keeps the same
  left edge as on a page without a back control

#### Scenario: Settings entry jump

- **WHEN** a visitor opens a settings entry at 800 pixels and then at 900 pixels
- **THEN** the entry heading does not move horizontally between the two widths

### Requirement: One breadcrumb component

The administration trail and the file browser trail SHALL both be drawn by `MdBreadcrumb`, as a `nav` with an ordered list. The
separator between two crumbs SHALL be an element with `aria-hidden="true"`, and no crumb SHALL draw its separator with a CSS pseudo
element. Overview SHALL draw the same shape as any other page.

#### Scenario: Separator is not a pseudo element

- **WHEN** the administration trail is drawn on a detail page
- **THEN** the separator is an element with `aria-hidden="true"` between two crumbs, and no element of the trail has an `after:` or
  `before:` class

#### Scenario: Overview trail

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the Overview page at 1440 pixels
- **THEN** the trail is the section name as its current crumb, drawn with the same classes as the other trails

### Requirement: Current crumb and narrow collapse

Exactly one crumb of a trail SHALL carry `aria-current="page"`, the last one. Below 1240 pixels the administration trail SHALL draw its
last crumb only, and the full trail SHALL be the `title` of that crumb. A long crumb SHALL truncate without breaking its separator.

#### Scenario: Current crumb

- **WHEN** the trail is drawn on any administration page
- **THEN** exactly one crumb carries `aria-current="page"`, and it is the last one

#### Scenario: Collapse below 1240 pixels

- **WHEN** a visitor opens a detail page at 900 pixels
- **THEN** the trail draws its last crumb only, and the full trail is in the `title` of that crumb

#### Scenario: Long crumb does not break the line

- **WHEN** a crumb carries a name longer than the trail's width
- **THEN** the crumb truncates with an ellipsis and its separator stays on the same line as the crumb before it


### Requirement: Detail panes share one inset

On a list-detail page the list pane and the detail pane SHALL take the same inset at every width. The detail pane SHALL NOT lose its inset
below 840 pixels.

#### Scenario: Teams at 390 pixels

- **WHEN** a visitor opens a team at 390 pixels
- **THEN** the team's heading starts at the same inset as the list's heading would at that width

#### Scenario: Principals at 1280 pixels

- **WHEN** a visitor opens a principal at 1280 pixels
- **THEN** the detail pane has the same inset as at 1920 pixels

### Requirement: Anchors keep their names

The dashboard SHALL keep every `data-ingot-component`, `data-ingot-part` and `data-ingot-state` name that `src/anchors/registry.json`
lists, and SHALL NOT rename or remove one. The registry SHALL gain the entry `administration-breadcrumb` for the administration trail,
registered as `experimental` in `src/components/layout/AdministrationFrame.vue`. The `header` part of `repository-detail` SHALL stay
registered and SHALL be drawn by an element in the repository detail's page content.

#### Scenario: Registry check

- **WHEN** `src/anchors/anchors.test.js` runs after the change
- **THEN** it passes, with `administration-breadcrumb` listed and no existing name missing

#### Scenario: Plugin stylesheet selector

- **WHEN** a plugin stylesheet in the `ingot-plugins` layer selects `[data-ingot-component="breadcrumb-navigation"]` on the file browser
- **THEN** the selector still matches the trail's `nav`

#### Scenario: Repository detail header part

- **WHEN** a visitor opens a repository detail
- **THEN** an element carries `data-ingot-part="header"` and `data-ingot-repository` with the repository name, and it holds the repository
  type section

### Requirement: Views use Tailwind utilities and tokens only

The views and primitives that this change touches SHALL style themselves with Tailwind utilities that resolve to the MD3 tokens. They
SHALL NOT contain a `<style>` block, a custom CSS class that is not a Tailwind utility or a token utility, an inline `style` binding for
a colour or a size, or a raw colour value.

#### Scenario: No custom class

- **WHEN** the touched files are read by the style gate of `add-ui-style-gate`
- **THEN** the gate reports no custom CSS class and no raw colour in them

#### Scenario: No style block

- **WHEN** the touched `.vue` files are searched for `<style`
- **THEN** no match is found
