Ingot is a lightweight repository manager for Maven artifacts.
It is a simple solution to replace managers like Nexus, Archiva or Artifactory.

Ingot is a fork of [Reposilite](https://github.com/dzikoysk/reposilite) by
[dzikoysk](https://github.com/dzikoysk) and contributors, licensed under the Apache License 2.0,
and continues from its 3.x line. Reposilite itself started out as NanoMaven.

Current site refers only to Ingot 3.x.
If you're looking for Reposilite 2.x documentation, take a look at:

* [Reposilite 2.x](https://v2.reposilite.com)
